# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Overview

The interactive **Chocolate Sales Analysis Dashboard** is developed using Power BI to analyze chocolate sales performance, product performance, shipment trends, geographical sales distribution, sales team performance, and sales target achievement.

The dashboard transforms sales and shipment data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, tables, and trend analysis. It helps stakeholders understand business performance, identify high-performing products and regions, evaluate sales personnel, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales revenue, total boxes sold, and shipment performance.
- Identify top-performing chocolate products based on sales and shipment metrics.
- Evaluate shipment trends over time.
- Compare sales performance across geographical regions.
- Analyze sales performance by sales personnel.
- Compare actual sales amounts against sales targets.
- Identify business opportunities to improve revenue and operational efficiency.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — KPI calculations, measures, and sales performance comparisons.
- **Data Modeling** — Organizing data tables and establishing relationships.
- **Data Visualization** — KPI cards, line charts, area charts, donut charts, bar charts, and tables.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Dashboard Overview

**Purpose:** Provide a consolidated view of overall chocolate sales and shipment performance.

#### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Amount | $44.69 Million |
| Total Boxes | 3.78 Million |
| Shipment Count | 7,905 |

#### Visualizations

- Total Amount and Amount per Shipment by Geography and Category.
- Shipment Trend over time.
- Sum of Boxes by Team.
- Total Boxes by Start of Month and Team.
- Geographical and sales-team performance summary.

#### Key Insights

- Generated approximately **$45 million in total sales**.
- Recorded approximately **4 million boxes** across **8K shipments**.
- The UK and India recorded the highest shipment counts among the six geographical markets displayed.
- Delish recorded the highest total box volume among the four sales teams, with approximately **1.147 million boxes**.
- Shipment volumes fluctuated over time, highlighting the importance of monitoring monthly logistics and sales activity.

### 2. Product & Shipment Analysis

**Purpose:** Evaluate product-level sales and shipment performance.

#### Key Metrics

- Total Amount.
- Shipment Count.
- Total Boxes.
- Boxes per Shipment.
- Amount per Shipment.

#### Visualizations

- Product-level sales and shipment performance table.
- Shipment Count by Geography bar chart.

#### Key Insights

- **Manuka Honey Choco** recorded approximately **$3.50 million** in sales.
- **Peanut Butter Cubes** generated approximately **$3.75 million** in sales.
- **Organic Choco Syrup** generated approximately **$2.79 million** in sales.
- The UK and India recorded the highest shipment counts, with approximately 1.4K shipments each.
- Canada recorded the lowest shipment count among the six geographical markets, at approximately 0.5K shipments.
- Comparing boxes per shipment and amount per shipment helps assess shipment efficiency and commercial value.

**Business Value:** Helps identify high-revenue products, understand shipping volumes, and evaluate opportunities to improve shipment efficiency.

### 3. Product & Sales Person Analysis

**Purpose:** Compare product performance and understand sales-person contributions to overall sales.

#### Analysis Areas

- Sales amount by product.
- Sales contribution by sales personnel.
- Product-level sales comparisons.
- Sales performance across the business.

#### Key Insights

- Product-level comparisons help identify products that contribute substantially to total revenue.
- Sales-person performance can be evaluated to identify high-performing employees and opportunities for improvement.
- Combining product and sales-person analysis helps businesses understand which products and sales activities contribute to revenue generation.

### 4. Sales Target & Sales Persons Analysis

**Purpose:** Evaluate individual sales-person performance against defined sales targets.

#### Key Metrics

- Sales Person.
- Total Amount.
- Sales Target.
- Target Comparison.

#### Visualizations

- Sales-person performance table.
- Actual sales amount compared with the sales target.
- Target achievement indicators using conditional icons.

#### Key Insights

- The report compares individual sales amounts against a sales target of **2,000,000 per sales person**.
- Sales personnel are classified according to whether they meet or exceed the defined target.
- Conditional indicators make it easier to identify employees who are meeting targets and those who may need additional support.
- The sales-person table supports performance monitoring and helps management identify opportunities for coaching and sales improvement.

### 5. Geographical Analysis

**Purpose:** Compare sales performance across six geographical markets.

#### Geographical Markets

- India
- United Kingdom
- Australia
- United States
- New Zealand
- Canada

#### Key Metrics

- Total Amount.
- Barr Amount.
- Barr Amount Percentage.
- Barr Bar Amount.

| USA | $7,896,249 | $653,587 | 8.3% |

#### Business Insights

- Canada recorded the lowest total sales, at approximately **$2.66 million**.
- Canada had the highest Barr Amount Percentage, at approximately **9.5%**, despite recording the lowest total sales.
